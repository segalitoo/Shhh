shhh.

A macOS dictation tool, designed to disappear.

I want to send a quick note
The problem

Voice input should feel invisible, as natural as typing.

Most dictation tools are clunky. They pop modal windows, break your flow, and feel bolted onto the OS. For bilingual users the gap is wider still: nothing auto-detects whether you are speaking English or Hebrew.

I wanted something that lives in the background, activates with a single key, and gets out of the way the moment you are done.

No setup ritual, no dashboard, no learning curve. You tap, you talk, the text lands where your cursor already is.

Design exploration

Finding the right indicator

Inspired by Apple's Dynamic Island, I drew four floating indicators, from information-dense to radically minimal. Then I lived with each one.
